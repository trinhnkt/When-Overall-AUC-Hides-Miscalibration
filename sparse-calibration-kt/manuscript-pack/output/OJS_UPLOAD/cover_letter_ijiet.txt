Cover letter — International Journal of Information and Education Technology

Manuscript: Reproducible Sparse-Concept and Calibration Diagnostics for Knowledge Tracing
Corresponding author: Van-Hau Nguyen (haunv@utehy.edu.vn)
Date: 24 September 2026

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript for IJIET. It is a protocol and
calibration diagnostic for knowledge-tracing (KT) probabilities that
educational platforms use to skip, remediate, or advance practice. The
evaluation uses train-only knowledge-component frequency strata,
occupancy-aware reporting, expected calibration error (ECE) and Brier
decomposition, reliability diagrams, and an L1–L7 leakage checklist. The
study is applied to three public logs (ASSISTments 2012, Junyi Academy,
and XES3G5M) with IRT, DKT, and a local Transformer reference (T-KT). A
locked probability threshold is reported only as a simulated
decision-error probe. The paper does not propose a new KT architecture,
a new calibrator, or a classroom intervention.

The named file is 13 pages in the IJIET template, including Appendix
Tables A1–A6 (alternative frequency cuts, the locked FAR probe, the full
Brier grid, the full leakage audit, and the locked T-KT τ-grid). The
double-blind file is 13 pages. All tables (main-text Tables 1–8 and
Appendix A1–A6) remain in this single Word manuscript; there is no
separate supplementary table file.
Please send reviewers only the blind PDF (and the blind Word file if
required) together with code_for_review_anonymous.zip. The named
Word/PDF are for the editorial office. Please do not send reviewers the
named files.

Exclusive submission. This manuscript is not under consideration
elsewhere. A prior submission of this work received an editorial
decision not to send the paper for peer review. The IJIET submission is

Funding. This work received no specific funding. It is not part of any

Data. The GitHub repository is private during review
(https://github.com/trinhnkt/Sparse-Concept-and-Calibration). Reviewers

acceptance, the repository will be made public and those accompanying-
artifact contents will be included there.

used to fabricate or alter experimental results. After using these
tools, the authors reviewed and edited the content. The authors remain
responsible for all content. Generative AI is not a co-author.
