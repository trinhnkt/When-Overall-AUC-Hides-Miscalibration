Manuscript: Reproducible Sparse-Concept and Calibration Diagnostics for Knowledge Tracing
Corresponding author: Van-Hau Nguyen (haunv@utehy.edu.vn)
Date: 29 September 2026

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript for IJIET. It is a protocol and
calibration diagnostic for knowledge-tracing (KT) probabilities that
educational platforms use to skip, remediate, or advance practice. The
evaluation uses train-only knowledge-component frequency strata,
occupancy-aware reporting, expected calibration error (ECE) and Brier
decomposition, reliability diagrams, and an L1–L7 leakage checklist. The
study is applied to three public logs (ASSISTments 2012, Junyi Academy,
and XES3G5M) with IRT, DKT, and a local Transformer reference (T-KT). A
locked probability threshold is reported only as a simulated
decision-error probe. The paper does not propose a new KT architecture,
a new calibrator, or a classroom intervention.

The named file is 13 pages in the IJIET template, including Appendix
Tables A1–A6 (alternative frequency cuts, the locked FAR probe, the full
Brier grid, the full leakage audit, and the locked T-KT τ-grid). The
double-blind file is 13 pages. All tables (main-text Tables 1–8 and
Appendix A1–A6) remain in this single Word manuscript; there is no
separate supplementary table file.
Please send reviewers only the blind PDF (and the blind Word file if
required) together with code_for_review_anonymous.zip. The named
Word/PDF are for the editorial office. Please do not send reviewers the
named files.

Ethics. This study is a secondary analysis of publicly released,
de-identified benchmark educational datasets (ASSISTments 2012, Junyi
Academy, and XES3G5M). No new human participants were recruited and no
classroom intervention was conducted. The Junyi Academy dump is used
under the provider's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. The authors processed only
the de-identified records released by the original dataset providers; no
direct identifiers were available to the authors, no re-identification
was attempted, and no new personal data were collected. Data handling
was conducted in accordance with applicable institutional and regional
data-protection requirements, including GDPR or FERPA where applicable
to the source data and jurisdiction.

Data. The artifact accompanying this submission contains configuration
files, preprocessing and audit scripts, frozen diagnostic summaries,
audit records, and a command that rebuilds the reported diagnostic
tables from those summaries. It does not include the raw public
datasets, historical training containers, or a guaranteed full retrain.
The artifact supports re-derivation of the reported tables, not
recreation of historical training-container commits. Source benchmark
datasets are publicly released by their original providers. During
review, reviewers receive only the anonymized accompanying artifact.
The public repository URL will be released upon acceptance. For the
editorial office only, and not for reviewers:
https://github.com/trinhnkt/Sparse-Concept-and-Calibration.
