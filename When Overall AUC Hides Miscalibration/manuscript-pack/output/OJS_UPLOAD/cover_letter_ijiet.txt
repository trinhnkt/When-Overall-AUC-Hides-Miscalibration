Cover letter — International Journal of Information and Education Technology

Manuscript: When Overall AUC Hides Miscalibration - Evaluating Knowledge Tracing on Rarely Practiced Knowledge Components
Corresponding author: Van-Hau Nguyen (haunv@utehy.edu.vn)
Date: 24 October 2026

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript for IJIET. It is a protocol and
calibration diagnostic for knowledge-tracing (KT) probabilities that
educational platforms use to skip, remediate, or advance practice. The
evaluation uses train-only knowledge-component frequency strata,
occupancy-aware reporting, expected calibration error (ECE) and Brier
decomposition, reliability diagrams, and eight leakage checks (L1–L8).
Check L8 withheld model-based results on XES3G5M. The study is applied
to three public logs (ASSISTments 2012, Junyi Academy, and XES3G5M)
with IRT, DKT, a local Transformer reference (T-KT), and simpleKT on
ASSISTments 2012. A locked probability threshold is reported only as a
simulated decision-error probe. The paper does not propose a new KT architecture,
a new calibrator, or a classroom intervention.

The named file is 12 pages in the IJIET template. The double-blind file
is 12 pages. All tables remain in this single Word manuscript: main-text
Tables 1–6 and Appendix Tables A1–A4 (Brier decomposition, alternative
frequency cuts, and the threshold probe). There is no separate
supplementary table file. Pre-specified validation-AUC checkpoint checks
are reported in the text and are not additional tables.
Please send reviewers only the blind PDF (and the blind Word file if
required) together with code_for_review_anonymous.zip. The named
Word/PDF are for the editorial office. Please do not send reviewers the
named files.

Ethics. This study is a secondary analysis of three publicly released,
de-identified educational benchmark datasets: ASSISTments 2012, Junyi
Academy, and XES3G5M. The records were de-identified by the original
dataset providers before release. The research team did not recruit new
participants, including students, teachers, or minors, did not contact
learners or their parents or guardians, and did not conduct a classroom
intervention. The team did not collect new written consent, new log
files, or video recordings. The research team did not collect new
personal data, access direct personal identifiers, or attempt to
re-identify any learner. The Junyi Academy dump is used under the
provider's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. The study is not an industry-partnered
data collection, and no EdTech company commissioned the analysis. Under
the institutional policy of Hung Yen University of Technology and
Engineering, this secondary analysis does not require prospective
human-subjects ethics review, and no institutional ethics protocol
number applies. Data handling was limited to those publicly released,
provider-de-identified records. Where the GDPR, FERPA, or another
regional data-protection law applies to the source data and its
jurisdiction, this handling is the stated basis of compliance.

Data. The artifact accompanying this submission contains configuration
